2. Awareness of Phishing Threats

Recently, our company has taken significant steps to recover from a security breach. This incident underscores the importance of staying vigilant against phishing attempts. Phishing emails are deceptive messages designed to trick you into providing sensitive information or downloading malicious software.

Common Indicators of Phishing Emails:

  • Suspicious Senders: Emails from unknown or mismatched domains.
  • Urgent Language: Requests that require immediate action, creating a sense of urgency.
  • Unexpected Attachments or Links: Files or links that you weren't anticipating.
  • Generic Greetings: Use of non-personalized salutations like "Dear Employee."

3. Upcoming Random Phishing Simulations

To enhance our collective cybersecurity awareness, we will be conducting random phishing simulations over the coming months. These simulated attacks are designed to help you recognize and respond effectively to real-world phishing threats.

What to Expect:

  • Random Timing: Phishing tests will be sent at various times without prior notice.
  • Varied Scenarios: Different types of phishing techniques will be employed to cover a broad range of potential threats.
  • Feedback and Training: If you identify a phishing attempt correctly, you will receive positive reinforcement. If not, constructive feedback and additional training will be provided.

Best Practices to Stay Safe:

  • Verify the Sender: Always check the sender's email address for authenticity.
  • Think Before You Click: Hover over links to see their actual destinations before clicking.
  • Report Suspicious Emails: Forward any suspicious emails to us immediately.
  • Use Strong Passwords: Combine letters, numbers, and special characters to create robust passwords, and update them regularly.
